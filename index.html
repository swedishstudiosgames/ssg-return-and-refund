Part 2: Your 30-Day "Matters of Taste" Cool-Off Rights (Buyer’s Remorse, Returns & Exchanges)

While Fourthwall does not accept general returns or sizing exchanges, SwedishStudios Games believes that "the customer is always right in matters of taste."

Therefore, You are entitled to cancel Your Order, request a refund, or request an exchange within a 30-day cool-off window for any reason—including buyer’s remorse, ordering the wrong size, or changing Your mind—subject to the strict conditions below.

1. Your Order Cancellation & Cool-Off Deadline

The deadline for requesting a return, exchange, or cancellation under Our Cool-Off Policy is 30 calendar days from the date on which You received the Goods (as verified by the carrier's delivery tracking) or on which a third party You have appointed, who is not the carrier, takes possession of the product delivered.

(Note: This 30-day window applies strictly from the delivery date of Your original Order. Receiving an exchanged or replacement item does not reset or extend the 30-day clock, and We allow a maximum of one (1) taste/sizing return or exchange per Order).

2. Mandatory Pre-Authorization & Receipt Requirement

In order to exercise Your right of cancellation, return, or exchange, You must first inform Us of Your decision and provide Your Receipt before shipping any Goods back. You can inform Us of Your decision and submit Your Receipt by:

  • By email: contact@swedishstudiosgames.com
  • By visiting this page on our website: https://contact.swedishstudiosgames.com

No Receipt, No Return: We will not authorize a return, exchange, or refund without a valid Receipt or verifiable Order number proving the Goods were purchased directly from Us.

3. Conditions for Returns (Closing the "Wardrobing" Loophole)

In order for the Goods to be eligible for a return or exchange under Our 30-day Cool-Off Policy, please make sure that:

  • Timely Delivery & Shipment: The Goods were delivered to You within the last 30 days, and once We approve Your return request, You ship and postmark the Goods back to Us within 14 calendar days of Our authorization.
  • Original Packaging & Tags: The Goods are in the original packaging with all original tags, labels, and hangtags still intact and attached.
  • Unworn & Unwashed Condition: The Goods are completely unworn (beyond a brief indoor try-on for fit), unwashed, un-altered, and free of stains, makeup, deodorant marks, smoke, perfume, or pet hair.
  • Receipt Included: A copy of Your Receipt or Order confirmation number is included inside the return package.

Right to Refuse or Deduct Depreciation: We physically inspect all returned Goods upon arrival. We reserve the right to refuse returns of any merchandise that does not meet the above return conditions in Our sole discretion (and return the item to You at Your expense), or to deduct a depreciation fee of up to 50% (or up to 100% if unsellable) if the Goods have been handled, worn, or degraded beyond what is necessary to establish their nature, characteristics, and functioning.

The following Goods cannot be returned under the 30-day "Matters of Taste" policy:

  • Hygiene & Unsealed Goods: The supply of Goods which are not suitable for return due to health protection or hygiene reasons (such as underwear, socks, swimwear, face masks, earrings, or drinkware) and were unsealed or used after delivery.
